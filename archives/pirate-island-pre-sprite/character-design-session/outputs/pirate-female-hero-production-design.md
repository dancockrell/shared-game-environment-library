## Hard review before improvement

The original five have useful, distinct fantasies, but the action contracts contain significant problems. Mara's ultimate appears to make nearby allies secure several objectives automatically. Inez's sideways velocity supposedly creates armor penetration, which neither the weapon nor the game needs. Pippa appears able to network every explosive in a room merely by using an ultimate. Tamsin has three overlapping takeover buttons, with unexplained universal access to every machine. Mercy reads hidden political facts from a glance and converts lethal damage into captivity after it has happened. Those promises undermine the otherwise coherent pirate faction.

The improved team performs five different jobs. Mara gets a crew to agree on the prize and offers terms when resistance is failing. Inez occupies overlooked real height. Pippa controls approach and escape with placed powder devices. Tamsin gets into a valuable machine, disables its safeguards and steals the actual object. Mercy turns wounded enemies into surviving prisoners who can be guarded, exchanged and potentially offered separate employment. None manufactures a prize, a prisoner, a recruit or an extra unit.

| Hero | Core decision | Winning situation | Honest weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mara | Is the next action worth risking the prize? | Wavering operators and a crew ready to exploit surrender | Loyal defenders with relief approaching |
| Inez | Which reachable high position gives one good shot? | Masts, cranes, rafters and roof edges | Flat terrain, wind, cut ropes and low ceilings |
| Pippa | Where must the enemy stop, turn or lose sight? | A prepared approach to a valuable intact objective | Wet charges, scattered enemies and rushed movement |
| Tamsin | Can this exact machine be disabled, accessed and extracted? | An isolated machine whose operator has lost control | Guarded controls, active denial systems and organic armies |
| Mercy | Can this person survive capture and the next hour? | Living wounded enemies and available guard capacity | Mindless attackers, area damage and too many casualties |

## Shared faction and party rules

All five are explicitly adult romance candidates, proposed ages 19–20, with heights within 4 feet 10 inches to 5 feet 3 inches. Their attraction, agency, histories and individual stories survive faction transfer. The main party is Michael plus at most four women. These five are a candidate pool; a five-woman pirate squad is a separate RTS formation, never a legal player-party composition.

Pirates recruit through a reachable offer of gold, shares, protection and opportunity to a dissatisfied living sapient person. A combat state cannot manufacture dissatisfaction, consent or employment. Captivity creates contact and denies the former faction a person's services, but does not change that person's allegiance. Michael-aligned living women are not purchasable, even in pirate custody. Cthulhu-controlled living people must regain sufficient agency before an employment decision; undead, animals and machines never enter a wage-recruitment decision. Romance with Michael is a separate relationship route, never a reward attached to paying ransom or applying treatment.

Capturing hardware follows the actual object's history: isolate, reach controls, disable safeguards, secure, crew, extract and maintain. Theft does not unlock manufacture. Original fuel, ammunition, damage, access requirements, specialist knowledge and recapture risks persist. An airship does not appear in a room because a hero once stole one. If a hero joins Michael, her physical abilities can help his current party, while pirate contracts cannot become a back door for recruiting men or generating human followers into Michael's faction.

Deaths obey the shared midnight pipeline. Living people who are downed remain living; dead people need completed faction-appropriate restoration before midnight. Stabilization, surrender, a white case or a custody record cannot suspend a corpse's deadline. A prisoner dying in custody remains the same identified person and may become an enemy at midnight.

Numerical values below are initial prototype tuning, not final balance. Distances are meters; cooldowns and durations use encounter simulation seconds. A normal direct hit is 1.0 damage. Normal pause freezes these timers. Strategic jobs use campaign time, real staff, real materials and physical assignments. If the game adopts a different action clock, convert the timings together. There is no separate invented hero-mana economy here; cooldowns, ammunition, supplies and attention are the limiting resources.

Party passives require membership in the deployed party and a conscious owner unless explicitly described as an already completed maintenance action. Strategic passives require the assignment and infrastructure stated. Equipment inspection benefits persist on inspected objects until their expiry or use; they are not magical remote auras. Captured, dead or absent heroes cannot perform work remotely. Duplicate identical bonuses do not stack.

References below to shared smoke, wind, traversal, surrender and custody systems are required integration contracts, not claims that those systems are implemented in the current native game. Temporary ability windows end their bonuses, not the existence of physical equipment: a deployed rope remains until retrieved, broken or otherwise explicitly removed. Its usable condition and attached endpoints must persist independently of its ability cooldown.

## Mara Quell — The Prize Captain

### Trope, personality and personal story

Mara is the smiling captain whose crew checks her arithmetic and finds that she has actually paid what she promised. She enjoys fine things, dangerous bargains and making a defeated officer admit that she has behaved better than his superiors. Her appetite for ownership can also make her reluctant to destroy something that is about to kill her crew. She is brave about her own skin and needs to become equally decisive about giving up a prize when other people's lives are the price.

Her story centers on a rich prize whose legal owner, working crew and rescue debt imply three incompatible claims. She cannot satisfy all three by delivering a stirring speech. The player can obtain records, trace cargo, recover an absent witness and decide what to surrender. Her credibility comes from accepting the cost of the articles she chose. Her romance with Michael tests whether sharing a life means giving someone a share of command, and whether either can accept a refusal without making it a war.

Proposed visual: age 20, 4 feet 11 inches, slim, olive skin, bright expressive face, black curls, practical tricorn, open sea-green captain's coat over a fitted short burgundy period waistcoat, narrow trousers and high boots. Her cutlass, heavy pistol, rolled articles and actual prize keys matter more than decorative coins. She is rich by selection, with a few excellent mismatched pieces, rather than covered in generic treasure.

### Basic, four powers, ultimate and passives

**Basic — Cutlass and Pistol.** At up to 7 meters, fire a 0.9-damage pistol shot using a loaded charge. At melee range, use a 1.8-meter cutlass cut for 1.0 damage. Weapon state is explicit: her carried pistol has one loaded shot, and reloading requires 2.5 uninterrupted seconds and actual ammunition. She does not silently get a fresh pistol every animation. When capture stance is enabled, a melee basic against a surrendering target is unavailable; the interaction becomes secure, release or order to move.

**Power 1 — Name the Prize.** Mark one visible person, reachable control, disabled machine, loot object or exit within 10 meters for 12 seconds. Allied capture/setup interactions on that exact object are 20% faster. A marked exit instead gives 15% faster traverse for allies using its existing path. It never increases lock proficiency or removes prerequisites. A person mark shows capture intent and enables ordinary cautious attack policies for controllable units, but it does not reduce incoming damage by magic. Limit one mark. Cooldown 10 seconds. Loss of sight retains the last-known marker without tracking hidden movement.

**Power 2 — Pistol Feint.** With a loaded pistol and an enemy within 3 meters, make a conspicuous aim followed by one controlled shot into the target's guarded space. The shot deals 0.5 damage if it hits and uses the normal ammunition. A sapient enemy may spend its normal response to guard or evade. If it does, Mara gains one optional 1.2-meter lateral step to valid ground and her next cutlass hit within 2 seconds gains 25% stagger. If it ignores her, she gains neither step nor bonus. Mindless attackers do not react to the feint, though the real shot can hit them. Cooldown 11 seconds. She cannot shoot past the guard as a harmless animation while pretending the bullet has no destination.

**Power 3 — Take Alive.** Attempt a close capture at 1.5 meters over a 1-second action. Eligible targets are living sapient enemies who are surrendering or downed, or who are at or below 20% health and presently staggered. Awake resisting targets also require Mara to have a conscious ally within 2 meters and no adjacent active enemy able to interfere. The move spends one restraint, stops bleeding through a basic dressing if available, and creates a restrained prisoner at that physical location. It deals no damage. It fails without spending a restraint if the target becomes ineligible before contact; interruption after restraint use leaves the consumed restraint on the ground or person according to the interrupted stage. Restraint alone does not guard, evacuate, heal or recruit. Cooldown 8 seconds.

**Power 4 — Terms on the Deck.** Speak an offer to visible living sapient enemies within 6 meters for 2 seconds. Only enemies currently considering surrender from ordinary morale/force/escape conditions make a decision; others continue fighting. A accepting enemy enters `Surrendering`, lowers its weapon and requests custody, but can resume resistance if attacked or left unsecured when its circumstances improve. Each enemy records this offer and cannot receive another from Mara for 20 seconds. No exact private loyalty or price is revealed. Cooldown 16 seconds. The offered terms are a selected existing policy—safe custody, release for arms, or ransom custody—and breaking a witnessed promise creates ordinary reputation consequences.

**Ultimate — All Hands Aboard.** Mark a 5-meter prize zone for 8 seconds and issue a coordinated boarding order to up to four nearby controllable allied units. They keep their bodies and current positions and follow normal paths; no one teleports. Each receives one explicit assignment, chosen from suppress operator, guard exit, secure surrendered person, or interact with a valid prize. Eligibility, equipment and target availability are previewed. Mara gains 20% stagger resistance while within the zone, and assigned allies gain 20% interaction speed on their specific job. Player-controlled companions retain manual override. No completed capture or takeover is guaranteed. A routed or incapacitated unit leaves its job visibly vacant. Cooldown 80 seconds.

**Party passive — Fair Share.** At a safe loot distribution, Mara's presence allows the party to record and settle promised shares immediately. Each applicable participant who actually receives the agreed share removes one ordinary grievance caused by that unresolved payment. It does not erase grief, ideology, romance conflict or fear of pointless danger. Gold is subtracted from the same loot ledger; the player's profit cannot include the money already paid. During combat this passive gives no unexplained morale aura.

**Strategic passive — The Articles.** While Mara oversees one pirate recruiting office, that office can include a verifiable shares-and-pay schedule in its offers. Candidates who value reliable payment apply the normal credibility component using her witnessed payment history. There is no universal acceptance bonus. A missed payment on her signed contract creates 25% more contract-related morale loss than an ordinary breach, an initial tunable downside. A recruitment offer remains invalid for Michael's loyal women. If Mara joins Michael, existing pirate contracts remain real obligations of their signatories, not assets transferred into his faction.

### Art and counterplay

Show the instant of physical possession: Mara's short balanced stance on the prize deck, cutlass pinning a surrendered officer's saber against the rail, free hand accepting an offered key, dangerous pleased smile. This distinguishes her opportunistic prize-taking from Rosamund's formal blockade and naval control. It also leaves her pistol holstered, avoiding impossible three-handed choreography.

Her weakest encounter has loyal defenders, no safe custody point and reinforcements arriving. Her ordinary pistol and cutlass still function, but she cannot make the battle profitable through a button. Cheap decoy objectives, dispersed enemies and a compromised payment reputation reduce her strategic strength.

## Inez Rake — The Rigging Ghost

### Trope, personality and personal story

Inez is a dry, observant marksman who feels calmer on a spar than in the middle of a field. She knows which knot a tired sailor tied badly and which officer never looks above a doorway. Her apparent vanishing is disciplined movement during other people's blind moments. She is not mystical. Her fear of exposed flat ground is embarrassing to her because everyone assumes a person who climbs so high must be fearless everywhere.

Her story forces her to help a stranded group across a place with no elevated refuge. Reaching an abandoned crane is not enough; she must bring the others to it and acknowledge that they cannot follow her usual route. A romance scene can reveal her choosing a vulnerable seat beside Michael rather than retreating to a perch where she controls every approach. Bravery is the choice, not a magically cured fear meter.

Proposed visual: age 20, 4 feet 10 inches, deep-brown skin, short tight curls, fine adult facial structure, narrow wiry build, charcoal short rigging coat over a striped fitted under-vest, visible waist, narrow salt-stained trousers and a properly loaded climbing harness. A long wood-stocked deck gun, grapnel, rope and small optical sight provide the identity. She should have believable boots, grip and harness pressure; the picture must not pretend gravity is optional.

### Exact kit

**Basic — Long Deck Gun.** A precise single shot up to 14 meters, 1.2 damage, followed by a 2.8-second reload using actual ammunition. A braced position occupied for 1 second reduces aim spread 20%. Movement or landing clears the brace and creates 0.7 seconds of increased aim spread. Elevation grants normal line-of-sight and cover advantages; it adds no automatic damage multiplier.

**Power 1 — Grapple Rise.** Select a visible authored high anchor within 7 meters connected to a validated climb line. Deploy one real rope and climb it at 2 meters per second. She cannot attack during the climb. Enemies can cut the rope at an accessible point or shoot the anchor. Broken support causes the ordinary fall/landing system to resolve her actual height; there is no forced cinematic survival. Anchors with no survivable landing can remain available only with a clear danger preview. The rope persists for 15 seconds and can be used by appropriately equipped actors from either side. Cooldown 12 seconds; one personal rope at a time.

**Power 2 — Pendulum Shot.** Using an existing compatible swing anchor and validated landing, Inez makes one short swing up to 6 meters. She may fire one loaded shot during a 0.5-second marked sight window near the slow part of the arc. The shot deals normal basic damage with ordinary cover resolution; its advantage is the briefly opened angle, never armor penetration created by movement. If no shot is chosen or the gun is empty, she completes the traversal. An invalid landing prevents activation. Cooldown 13 seconds. This power requires a deliberately supported swing animation and can remain disabled until that animation and collision are verified.

**Power 3 — Drop the Yard.** Interact with one visible authored overhead load control within 2 meters. A prepared spar, net, sail bundle or cargo load falls into its displayed landing area after a 1-second tell. The object is consumed or changes to a persistent fallen state; the power cannot conjure another. Light netting restrains movement until cut; a heavy spar deals its ordinary environmental damage and can kill friend or enemy. Load type and friendly danger are shown before use. No valid environmental load means the power is unavailable. Cooldown 8 seconds limits repeated controls, but the real scarcity is the physical scenery.

**Power 4 — Smoke Above.** Drop one carried smoke pot to a valid ground surface within 4 meters below or beside her. It produces a 3-meter smoke volume for 6 seconds after a 0.7-second tell. It obstructs normal sight for both sides and disperses according to the existing wind/smoke model. It does not silence shots, remove tracking marks, erase footsteps or grant invisibility. Initial carried supply two pots. Cooldown 16 seconds. A pot cannot land through a sealed floor.

**Ultimate — No Safe Deck.** Choose up to three existing reachable high anchors with a total valid climb/swing path of at most 15 meters. Inez traverses them in order and can make one deliberate shot from each reached brace. She must start with sufficient loaded weapons or spend the actual reload time between shots; the ability supplies no free cartridges. For up to 12 seconds, bracing time is reduced to 0.4 seconds and aimed shots gain 25% stagger against exposed operators. A broken anchor cancels the unreached remainder; completed shots and her current location persist. She is vulnerable while moving. Cooldown 85 seconds.

**Party passive — Look Up.** While conscious, Inez adds an elevated scan to the party's local perception. Actual moving climbers or rooftop actors within 12 meters and unblocked vertical sight can be detected 0.5 seconds sooner than the party's normal scan interval. The presentation gives an accessible directional cue and uncertain last-seen marker. It does not see through roofs, identify disguised people or query all enemy positions.

**Strategic passive — High Routes.** While assigned to a staffed ropewalk, Inez can survey and equip one existing vertical route job at a time. The job consumes rope, anchors and worker time and enables a compatible authored graph edge for actors with the relevant climbing tags. Both endpoints and their physical access remain required. Hostile capture, cut rope or a collapsed structure can disable it. This is maintained infrastructure that can be used by an enemy who reaches and understands it, not a faction-exclusive teleport link.

### Art and counterplay

Her first art should show a supported firing position: one boot braced on a yard or beam, loaded harness tied to a visible anchor, long gun properly shouldered toward guards below. A full airborne swing-shot is a later animation challenge, not required to prove her identity. She is looking at the shot, not the viewer.

Her dominance falls away on a flat beach. Guns still work there, but her traversal and scenery powers depend on actual features. Shoot her anchor, occupy her next perch, obstruct the landing, force her to reload under pressure or exploit her predictable climb path.

## Pippa Cinder — The Powder Saint

### Trope, personality and personal story

Pippa respects powder with a seriousness that other pirates find funny until they see a damaged magazine. She is intensely alive to small practical details: a damp seam, a dropped ember, someone putting a warm tool where it does not belong. Her humor is precise and unexpectedly sharp. She likes well-made, slightly ridiculous containers and refuses to let anyone confuse fearlessness with carelessness.

Her story concerns a captured magazine everyone wants to sell immediately. The accounts imply it was sabotaged before the pirates arrived, and exposing the truth may destroy the only profitable cargo the crew has taken in weeks. She needs evidence and time, while every faction wants the powder. Her romance with Michael grows through shared making, argument over acceptable risk and the relief of trusting someone else to finish a delicate job. It does not require her to become a reckless explosives caricature.

Proposed visual: age 19, 5 feet tall, slim freckled adult, cropped copper hair, large attentive eyes with adult proportions, mustard short powder coat with one scorched tail, fitted dark vest, narrow trousers, boots and proper gloves. Ceramic powder pots, separately stored fuse reels and smoke containers establish the kit. Keep the clutter subordinate to her hands and the single active device.

### Exact kit

**Basic — Spark Pistol.** A 6-meter single pistol shot, 0.8 damage, with 2.5-second reload and actual ammunition. A hit may ignite an exposed object explicitly tagged `ImpactIgnitable`; this is an authored game property, not a promise that all powder, oil, metal or rope ignites when shot. Targeting an explosive displays its known danger area and can harm allies through normal environmental resolution.

**Power 1 — Smoke Orange.** Throw one carried smoke charge to visible reachable ground within 6 meters. After 0.7 seconds it makes a 3-meter orange smoke volume for 6 seconds. The boundary has a shape cue as well as color. Both sides lose sight through the thick center; already tracked positions remain uncertain memories. Wind displaces/disperses it through the shared smoke system. Carry three smoke charges initially, replenished from stores. Cooldown 10 seconds.

**Power 2 — Thunder Mug.** Place one directional powder pot at adjacent ground with a displayed 60-degree, 4-meter frontal effect cone and a 1-meter immediate danger radius. Placement takes 1.2 seconds. It remains inert until its physical fuse is lit, an authored ignition hit lands, or an enemy deliberately ignites it. On detonation, the cone deals 1.1 damage and 1-second stagger to exposed light actors; heavy actors get 0.3 seconds. The immediate radius threatens everyone, and hard cover blocks the cone normally. It is not intrinsically safe behind itself. Initial capacity two pots; limit two active placed devices. A detonated pot is consumed and leaves debris. Cooldown 8 seconds governs placing, not free resupply.

**Power 3 — Running Fuse.** While moving normally, lay up to 5 meters of physical fuse between two reachable points, consuming one carried fuse length. It may connect to one placed compatible device or an already inspected exposed environmental ignition point. After placement, either end can be lit by an adjacent interaction or valid ignition source. Burning advances on the visible path at the game's fixed fuse speed; cutting or wetting an accessible unburned section stops propagation. No remote ignition across a gap. Maximum three personal fuse links, each placed through a separate action and paid from actual supply. Cooldown 8 seconds; maximum three carried lengths. Exact real-world chemical composition is outside the game design and unnecessary.

**Power 4 — Flash the Hold.** Throw one low-fragment flash pot at visible ground within 5 meters. After a 0.8-second warning it flashes in a 3-meter radius, giving facing living actors 1.2 seconds of disrupted aim and a 0.5-second interrupt. Actors behind solid cover, outside sensory exposure, or lacking the relevant senses are unaffected. It does little structural damage and no direct health damage, but does not guarantee surrender. It affects allies under the same exposure rule. Initial capacity two; cooldown 14 seconds. Photosensitivity presentation can replace the bright flash with a desaturated visual cue without changing simulation timing.

**Ultimate — Saint's Chain.** Pippa prepares a maximum of three compatible existing devices through actual connected fuse links before activation. With her initial two carried Thunder Mugs, a third device must be an already inspected compatible environmental object or a separately supplied allied charge, never an extra conjured pot. Preview the reachable ignition sequence and all known danger zones. The ultimate gives one 1-second ignition action and, for 8 seconds, permits her to choose the next prepared branch at an accessible junction without a separate handling delay. Flames still travel along real intact fuses; enemies can disrupt or ignite them. It does not connect foreign charges automatically, detonate all explosives in the room, produce new supplies or retroactively spare valuables. Cooldown 75 seconds. A room with no prepared devices yields no ultimate target.

**Party passive — Dry Powder.** At rest, with packaging supplies, Pippa may seal up to five carried ammunition containers belonging to the party. Each sealed container ignores its first ordinary environmental wetting check, then loses the seal. It provides no explosion immunity and does not keep submerged powder dry forever. The protection is recorded on the actual container and can persist if Pippa later leaves; the party must pay and pause to reseal it.

**Strategic passive — Magazine Discipline.** While assigned to a staffed powder store, Pippa inspects one stored magazine batch per normal work cycle. An inspected batch has one unsafe-storage condition identified and can be corrected by staff using actual materials. Properly corrected storage reduces that specific accident cause rather than applying universal random luck. Visible known blast-zone previews are information available to anyone who has inspected the layout. This passive cannot turn captured unsafe powder into immediate sellable cargo without work.

### Art and counterplay

Her keyframe is a carefully placed directional pot with an empty threat cone ahead, a long fuse visibly running toward shelter, and Pippa checking the setup while indicating retreat. Hands, fuse and escape direction must agree. She should not kneel inside an imminent blast or surround friends with burning grenades. The scene can be tense and sexy through competence and expression without presenting carelessness as her personality.

Pippa creates a route to an intact prize. Beatrix's commandos overwhelm a breach through explosive confusion and desperate momentum. Pippa waits, places, diverts and detonates for a particular result; she does not inherit Beatrix's frenzy. Forced relocation, rain affecting exposed material, multiple enemy approaches and premature ignition are her natural counters.

## Tamsin Key — The Machine Thief

### Trope, personality and personal story

Tamsin is the mechanic who sees a splendid locked engine and immediately wonders which frightened operator hid the spare handle. She studies how a machine is kept obedient, not how its inventor derived it. She is funny, nosy and delighted by shortcuts, sometimes too willing to call a stolen device fixed when it is merely moving in the desired direction. Michael's inventions fascinate her because they make that distinction dangerous.

Her story starts with a valuable stolen machine whose damaged fail-safe is protecting its former crew from a larger disaster. Keeping the prize, surrendering it or finishing the repair each carries a real cost. Her romance with Michael can develop through intellectual attraction and an argument over authorship, ownership and trust: she must eventually ask to open something that she could probably steal. This does not require him to give her all his designs or unlock a manufacturing tree.

Proposed visual: age 20, 5 feet 2 inches, very slim, a clever angular adult face, dark bobbed hair, cropped oilskin work jacket over a fitted rust-red wrap, reinforced narrow trousers, secure tool harness and boots. Her pick, hooked chain, control handles and physical service panel define her. Favor steam valves, mechanical governors and keyed linkages appropriate to the setting. The title Cross the Wires does not justify modern touchscreen hacking.

### Machine access model

A stealable asset has a persistent ID, original provenance, current physical custodian, control owner, operator seats, movement state, weapons, fuel/ammunition, integrity, access points, control compatibility, denial mechanisms and extraction requirements. Custody and powered control are separate: pirates can own a helpless prize that they cannot yet operate. Character operators remain people and never transfer allegiance with the machine.

Control takeover uses bounded states: `Contested`, `MotionDisabled`, `AccessExposed`, `SafeguardResolved`, `LocallyControlled`, `Secured`, `Extracted`. Some assets can skip a physical step because it has already been completed; none can skip required safeguards because an ultimate was pressed. Capturing a specific key grants only its recorded scope. Magical attunement, remote shutdown and self-denial use explicit authored locks with known access conditions, not unbounded hacking minigames.

### Exact kit

**Basic — Wrecker's Pick.** A 1.5-meter strike, 0.75 damage to people and ordinary structure. Against an exposed control linkage it deals 1.25 units of control-integrity damage while dealing only 0.4 structural damage. The valid weak point must already be exposed or discovered through close inspection. It does not know every machine's secret weakness automatically.

**Power 1 — Kill the Motion.** Attach a hooked chain to one visible eligible wheel, limb linkage, carriage or tiller within 3 meters. A 0.6-second throw/attach action blocks that tagged movement subsystem for up to 4 seconds while leaving other subsystems active. The chain can be removed by a 1-second adjacent interaction or broken by sufficient torque/damage according to the asset's rated strength. Heavy machinery may merely slow 25% rather than stop. One reusable chain, one target; it remains an actual retrievable object if broken loose. Cooldown 12 seconds begins after recovery. No effect on organic limbs, spirits or inaccessible internal mechanisms.

**Power 2 — Borrow the Key.** Spend 1.5 uninterrupted seconds taking a loose key/control handle within reach, searching a secured consenting or captured operator, or inspecting a reachable unlocked panel for a compatible bridge point. A resisted living person must first be disarmed/secured; the power is not magical pickpocketing during combat. Success produces a persistent access token with exact asset/subsystem scope or the finding `No compatible access`. Copying requires a suitable consumable blank and an actual later work action. Cooldown 6 seconds. The token opens an access layer, not ownership, fuel or expertise.

**Power 3 — Cross the Wires.** At an exposed reachable panel, with a compatible token/tool and no active operator contesting the control, channel for 3 seconds to override exactly one eligible subsystem for 6 seconds: movement stop/start, one door/lift command, signal mode, or a limited manual weapon command. A weapon command still needs ammunition, safe arc, an operator at its controls and a valid target. Tamsin remains at the panel and can be interrupted. Denial or remote shutdown can terminate the override. It is not whole-asset transfer. Cooldown 18 seconds; incompatible magical or unfamiliar controls report the unmet requirement rather than yielding a random success roll.

**Power 4 — Strip Her Clean.** While adjacent to an immobilized or unpowered machine, select one discovered portable component and channel for 4 seconds using its required tool. The component moves into actual carry inventory if capacity permits; the donor machine permanently loses its recorded function until repaired. Fuel cells, control handles and ammunition boxes have different weight/hazard tags. Operators or guards can interrupt. A removed key component cannot remain installed as a second copy. Heavy inaccessible components require normal salvage jobs and are not valid. Cooldown 12 seconds.

**Ultimate — Change of Ownership.** Begin a three-stage takeover at an eligible machine: establish physical control, resolve its known remaining safeguard, then secure its operating station. Each stage takes an initial 2 seconds and requires uninterrupted adjacent work plus the displayed tools/operator conditions. A stage already completed physically is checked rather than repeated. Tamsin receives 25% resistance to minor action disruption while working, but hard interruption, active guards or an operator contest halt progress. Completion sets control/custody to the captor's faction only if all actual requirements are met. The same damaged asset must still be crewed, fueled and extracted. Failed progress persists only where a real panel, lock or restraint changed. Cooldown 90 seconds starts on the first completed stage. This is faster coordinated capture, not immunity to safeguards.

**Party passive — Useful Parts.** After Tamsin spends one ordinary inspection action at a disabled device, its known removable controls, ammunition and compatible components become explicit salvage choices for the party. ## Mercy Vane — The Ransom Surgeon

### Trope, personality and personal story

Mercy is terrifyingly polite because she intends the person under her hands to survive long enough to repeat her exact words. Her commercial reasoning and her compassion are both sincere. She dislikes waste, sloppy cruelty and patients who pretend not to be frightened. Her ability to make a captive laugh while checking a bandage is a powerful part of her attraction; so is her willingness to confront her own crew over treatment.

Her story involves a prisoner whose family cannot or will not meet a promised ransom. Maintaining custody is becoming expensive, and several people want the captive dead for unrelated reasons. The player can verify claims, arrange exchange, negotiate release or provide an alternative payment, but cannot create money from a hidden ransom stat. Mercy must decide whether her humane reputation survives when kindness stops being profitable. Michael's romance route asks whether she can trust care that is offered without keeping a ledger of what it buys.

Proposed visual: age 20, 5 feet 1 inch, slim, warm brown eyes, long dark hair under a narrow sea hat, fitted white-and-black surgeon's coat with teal lining, short lace-up period waistcoat, narrow trousers and boots. A medical satchel, simple dressing, keys and clean restraint communicate surgeon and custodian. Her clothing should read as a pirate medical professional, with no fetish-nurse shorthand. Her patient remains a person, not a decorative trophy.

### Capture and treatment state model

Living people can be standing, staggered, downed, surrendering, restrained, guarded, transported or released. Health, bleeding and restraint are separate fields; restrained does not mean medically stable, and stabilized does not mean captured. A dead actor stays dead until the global restoration system completes. Every restraint and guard assignment is counted; an unguarded restrained prisoner can attempt escape according to actual capacity and circumstances.

Mercy's care can create trust, but reputation changes are witnessed events and memories rather than global automatic truth. An enemy may ignore a white case. An ally may break promised treatment. These choices can have consequences without producing supernatural pacifism or a combat-wide legal shield.

### Exact kit

**Basic — Lancet Pistol.** A small pistol shot at up to 6 meters deals 0.7 damage and uses actual ammunition, with a 2.3-second reload. At close range she can use a 1.2-meter defensive instrument strike for 0.5 damage. Neither ordinary hit magically makes a target nonlethal or physiologically easier to abduct. **Power 1 — White Flag Case.** Place her real medical case on adjacent ground over 0.8 seconds, opening a 2.5-meter treatment area for up to 12 seconds. **Power 2 — Bind the Wound.** At 1.5 meters, spend one dressing and channel for 2 seconds on a living willing, surrendered or physically secured person. Restore 5% maximum health only if the existing wound permits it; this never raises a corpse or resolves a critical wound. A resisting person is not a valid treatment target until contained. Restraint is a separate optional follow-up costing its own supply and action. Cooldown 7 seconds. Initial carried dressings five.

**Power 3 — Sleeping Measure.** Fire one carried prepared dart at a visible unarmored living sapient target within 4 meters. Exact pharmacology and manufacture are outside this fictional mechanic.

**Power 4 — Know Your Price.** Spend a 1.5-second observation or conversation action on a visible sapient person within 4 meters who can be heard. **Ultimate — Everyone Leaves Alive.** For 8 seconds, set a capture plan for up to four controllable allied actors within a 5-meter area. Damage remains damage: bullets, explosions, critical wounds and uncontrolled enemies can still kill. No lethal blow is retroactively rewritten and no creature is automatically secured. Invalid targets are excluded. Manual player attack overrides remain possible and visibly break the capture plan. Cooldown 85 seconds.

**Party passive — Worth More Breathing.** With Mercy deployed, party capture stance can preview whether a selected direct attack is likely to kill an already weakened eligible target and provide a dedicated restraint/disarm alternative where valid. Damage-over-time and area attacks remain dangerous. The player can deliberately override with a clear hostile action.

**Strategic passive — Good Custody.** While assigned to a staffed brig/recovery house, Mercy can supervise one additional health inspection per normal care cycle. Food, clean space, dressing supplies and guards must actually exist. Identified illness, untreated bleeding and unsafe restraint create explicit care jobs; completed jobs reduce their corresponding death or escape risks. Ransom credibility follows actual surviving releases and kept terms. Her title grants no automatic ransom-value multiplier, no perfect interrogation, and no immunity to a midnight outbreak after a prisoner's death.
