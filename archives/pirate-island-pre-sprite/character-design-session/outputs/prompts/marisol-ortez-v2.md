Replace the conventional saddle lean with a credible off-side cavalry hang: one boot locked in a period stirrup, bent leg crossing the saddle, body low on the opposite side beneath the horse's neck while one hand controls the rein. Replace the vertical launcher-like tube with a horizontal leather dispatch case containing sealed messages. Keep rope, horse-side saber, period riding spencer, bodice, high-waisted wool trousers, boots, neckerchief, adult age eighteen-to-nineteen, short lean small-busted build. No modern weapon, denim, cowboy cosplay, floating anatomy, text, UI, logo, or watermark.
