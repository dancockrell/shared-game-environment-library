# Pirate Island

## Provisional Female Hero Designs for the Four Remaining Factions

**Status:** Character-first concept pass for art generation and approval. These women are not yet accepted design authority or installed character photographs. Faction labels remain descriptive; no proper faction names are introduced.

Each hero is an adult woman between approximately 4 feet 10 inches and 5 feet 3 inches. The female hero and major-notable cast should cluster tightly around an apparent age of eighteen to twenty, with the exact age assigned character by character. The approved global direction remains: unmistakably adult presentation, compact proportions, slim or lithe builds, predominantly small-busted or flat-chested, cute-to-beautiful faces, strong individual silhouettes, modern color graphic-novel rendering, and adventurous edge-of-PG-13 clothing without nudity or child coding.

Age is a continuity constraint, not a same-face template. A character's profession, faction, temperament, health, experience, expression, grooming, posture, movement, costume wear, and equipment must create the visible differences that age alone cannot. Young officers and experts should look young without looking inexperienced, juvenile, or generically interchangeable.

Each kit contains a basic action, one party passive, one strategic passive, four encounter powers, and an ultimate. Recruitment systems remain strategic mechanics rather than encounter-power substitutions.

# 1. Colonial Powers: Five Female Heroes

## 1.1 Beatrix Calder - The Breach Captain

**Portrait status:** Version 10 is the approved character image. It fixes Beatrix as an unmistakably adult woman of approximately nineteen, with a compact silhouette, period-derived interior bodice and short cavalry breeches, readable revolver-and-saber kit, cute face, and controlled battle-lust expression. This is the stable identity reference for later Beatrix art.

![Beatrix Calder - approved character image](assets/characters/beatrix-calder.png)

**Rejected portrait direction:** Version 3 is rejected. A jacket paired with modern-looking shorts, a ceremonial extended-arm stance, and generic rubble did not communicate Beatrix's job. It must not be used as a costume, pose, or semantic reference for later work.

**Combat-plate candidate:** beatrix-calder-v13 is the current proposed revision for review. Sword-led breach advance; shorter step; fresh blast among friendlies replaced with smoke. Approved face and costume retained. Version 10 remains the approved identity image.

![Beatrix Calder - grenade breach combat plate](assets/characters/provisional/beatrix-calder-v13.png)

**Trope and narrative:** Beatrix is the officer who goes through the door first. She inherited a commission everyone assumed was decorative, then took command when a redoubt collapsed and its senior officers froze. She is neither a speechmaker nor a remote general. She identifies the point that must break, organizes the opening, enters during the lethal first seconds, fixes the route inside, and turns a dangerous hole into a controlled path for the assault file behind her. Her authority comes from repeatedly accepting the danger she orders others to face.

**Attached formation - sacrificial breach commandos:** Beatrix leads rough adult men selected or volunteering for exceptionally dangerous close assault. They abandon most uniform outerwear in the island heat but retain visibly period interior garments, shirts, waistcoats, braces, cut-down cavalry trousers, crossbelts, and boots. Their standard assault equipment is several black-powder fuse grenades, a brace of percussion pistols or cap-and-ball revolvers, and a short sword or heavy hanger. A red guide cord fixed with a plain iron breach spike makes their route legible through smoke. Their doctrine is grenade saturation followed immediately by a human rush: throw into the defended interior, let smoke, concussion, splinters, noise, horror, and broken formation steal the defenders' first coherent response, then cross before that response returns. The commandos deliberately enter a short battle frenzy because going forward violently is their only plausible way out. Their purpose is to create and seize an entrance, displace the first defenders, and hold the threshold long enough for an ordinary formation to pass; they are not general-purpose skirmishers or line infantry.

**Basic action - Officer's Pair:** Fire one measured revolver shot at range or make a fast saber cut in close quarters. The action changes automatically with distance.

**Party passive - Through Smoke:** Party members who cross Beatrix's fixed route shortly after an explosion gain a short brace against fear, pain, stagger, and suppression. The protection ends if they stop advancing or leave the route.

**Strategic passive - Credible Command:** Colonial patrols that see Beatrix survive a dangerous engagement recover morale and reform more quickly. Units ordered into a battle she personally joins are less likely to rout, but her defeat causes an unusually visible morale shock.

**Power 1 - Fix the Route:** Drive or throw an iron breach spike into a valid point and pull the red guide cord taut. It defines one short assault route through smoke and debris; allies following it path cleanly, while enemies and breakable defenses on the line become easier to stagger or breach.

**Power 2 - Grenade the Dark:** Order a timed volley of period fuse grenades into a doorway, room, trench, or defended patch. The blasts deal moderate explosive damage but are principally for concussion, smoke, guard break, and formation disorder. Short fuses and confined spaces make bad placement dangerous to allies.

**Power 3 - Pistol and Point:** Fire at one target, dash to a second nearby target, and deliver a saber thrust. If both actions strike the same target, the thrust breaks guard instead.

**Power 4 - Wolf's Chance:** Beatrix and nearby followers enter a brief deliberate frenzy. They advance and attack faster, ignore the first fear, pain, or suppression interruption, and gain close-assault impact; during the effect they lose ranged discipline, turn more slowly toward threats outside the route, and cannot begin an orderly disengagement.

**Ultimate - Through the Gap:** Beatrix commits the marked route. A grenade sequence walks just ahead of the assault, then she charges through enemies or breakable cover and followers may immediately enter behind her. Each broken blocker carries the frenzy forward, but the formation cannot reverse until it reaches the route endpoint, Beatrix falls, or the assault is manually broken at severe morale cost.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** She converts a party into a one-direction assault formation and makes one decisive opening rather than providing universal buffs. The grenade smoke and induced frenzy create seconds of extraordinary local superiority, not safety. Open ground, several approach angles, premature detonations, resistant defenders, blocked endpoints, friendly units caught ahead of the blast, and an enemy that survives long enough to restore formation can turn the commitment into a massacre.

**Visual identity:** A compact, cute-faced nineteen-year-old officer in a fitted ivory interior bodice, short period cavalry breeches, stockings and garters, tall practical boots, revolver, cavalry saber, grenade satchel, iron breach spike, and red guide cord. There is no flag or ceremonial standard. Controlled pinned hair, direct gaze, powder wear, and a dangerous private smile sell the contradiction: visually desirable and very young, but already the person most willing to enter the worst point first.

## 1.2 Odette Mercer - The Battery Architect

**Concept-art status:** odette-mercer-v3 is the current proposed revision for review. Visible sight alignment and carriage adjustment, low braid, simplified tools and clearer sea light. Equipment remains illustrative and requires a separate construction study.

![Odette Mercer - Battery Architect concept-art candidate](assets/characters/provisional/odette-mercer-v3.png)

**Trope and narrative:** Odette sees a battlefield as angles, timing, masonry, and consequences. She began as a surveyor and became an artillery officer after proving that a supposedly impregnable fort had been built around the wrong sight lines. She carries enough of a battery with her to make geometry immediately dangerous, but her strongest work still depends on preparation and protecting the equipment she deploys.

**Basic action - Survey Carbine:** A precise carbine shot whose accuracy improves against targets already measured or stationary.

**Party passive - Known Range:** The party's first ranged attack against a clearly observed enemy suffers less distance and elevation penalty.

**Strategic passive - Prepared Emplacements:** Colonial-controlled nodes build firing positions and establish artillery arcs faster when Odette has surveyed the connected route. Losing her survey records removes the benefit until the work is repeated.

**Power 1 - Sight the Line:** Place two survey stakes to define a firing line. Targets crossing it are revealed and become easier for projectile attacks to lead.

**Power 2 - Folding Wall Gun:** Assemble a compact crew-served swivel gun on a tripod. It delivers powerful narrow fire but cannot move or rotate freely until packed again.

**Power 3 - Canister Fan:** Load the wall gun or her heavy hand piece with canister and fire a short, brutal cone that knocks light enemies out of formation.

**Power 4 - Powder Train:** Lay a visible fuse between Odette and a charge placed on cover, a door, a machine, or terrain. The fuse can be cut or ignited early by either side.

**Ultimate - Calculated Bombardment:** Use her range board to designate three sequential impact zones. The salvos land with readable delay, destroying prepared positions and forcing enemies through the safe geometry Odette intended.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Odette creates strong firing problems and rewards preplanned lanes. Fast flankers, broken visibility, premature detonation, destroyed stakes, and pressure during setup deny much of her value.

**Visual identity:** A very slim adult woman in a fitted russet engineer's coat with rolled sleeves, pale field shirt, dark cropped vest, survey harness, rangefinder, stakes, powder satchel, carbine, and a folded tripod gun carried with believable straps and load paths. Inked calculations on one cuff; soot rather than ornamental brass.

## 1.3 Marisol Ortez - The Dispatch Rider

**Concept-art status:** marisol-ortez-v4 is the current proposed revision for review. Seated turning shot replaces the contorted hanging pose. Larger expressive face, swinging braid, greener environment and sealed dispatch case strengthen her identity. The carbine now points toward the visible pursuers; mounted animation still requires its own pose study.

![Marisol Ortez - Dispatch Rider concept-art candidate](assets/characters/provisional/marisol-ortez-v4.png)

**Trope and narrative:** Marisol is the frontier courier who arrives after everyone else has concluded the route is impossible. She grew up carrying orders between isolated settlements and distrusts officers who confuse a map with the ground. Her horse is a living partner rather than a speed statistic. Indoors or after separation she remains a complete agile carbine fighter, but in open rooms the pair can turn distance into initiative.

**Basic action - Saddle Carbine:** A quick carbine shot that can be fired during movement with reduced penalty.

**Party passive - Never Late:** The party gains a short burst of movement when a timed objective, retreat window, or endangered ally first becomes urgent.

**Strategic passive - Living Dispatch:** Orders carried through routes Marisol has personally ridden update allied AI priorities faster and with less information loss than ordinary signals.

**Power 1 - Spur Through:** Mounted, Marisol rides through a selected lane and knocks enemies aside; dismounted, she performs a low sprint and shoulder roll through the same space.

**Power 2 - Saddle Shot:** Move and fire without stopping. The shot gains power if Marisol crosses the target's facing before releasing it.

**Power 3 - Dragoon Loop:** Throw a rope loop that pulls a light enemy, arrests a fleeing unit, or anchors Marisol against forced movement.

**Power 4 - Change the Ground:** Vault over low cover or from saddle to elevation, leaving her horse to circle toward a safe rally point. Reactivation recalls the horse when a route exists.

**Ultimate - Ride the Whole Line:** Make a sequence of rapid passes through chosen points, firing at one marked target on each pass and delivering nearby allies a momentary movement order as she crosses them.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Marisol links distant problems and turns open geometry into repeated attacks. Narrow rooms, blocked routes, hazards, separated mount access, roots, and enemies who predict her return line reduce her advantage.

**Visual identity:** A short, lean adult woman with sun-browned skin, animated dark eyes, a cropped sand-colored riding jacket over a fitted burgundy waistcoat, scarf, narrow riding trousers, high boots, carbine, rope, dispatch case, and light cavalry saber. Portrait composition includes her alert non-mechanical horse as a real character without hiding Marisol's full silhouette.

## 1.4 Doctor Clara Morrow - The Red-Hand Surgeon

**Concept-art status:** clara-morrow-v3 is the current proposed revision for review. Both hands treat a covered wound; pistol holstered. Warm attention to the patient and lantern-versus-rain lighting replace the earlier blind firing and one-handed surgery.

![Doctor Clara Morrow - Red-Hand Surgeon concept-art candidate](assets/characters/provisional/clara-morrow-v3.png)

**Trope and narrative:** Clara is the physician who refuses to let administration decide who is already dead. She is gentle with frightened patients, ruthless with contamination, and terrifyingly calm while bullets are still passing through the room. The red staining her gloves is work, not horror decoration. She keeps people alive through procedures the party can see and enemies can interrupt.

**Basic action - Service Pistol:** A controlled pistol shot; at adjacent range Clara instead uses a fast scalpel cut that applies a brief anatomical vulnerability.

**Party passive - Clean Instruments:** Bleeding, infection, and injury complications accumulate more slowly while Clara remains supplied and conscious.

**Strategic passive - Case Ledger:** Hospitals connected to Clara's records identify casualties, preserve names, and route bodies more reliably before midnight. Destroyed records remove the advantage.

**Power 1 - Clamp:** Immediately stop bleeding on an adjacent ally or clamp a vulnerable point on an adjacent humanoid enemy, briefly weakening that limb's attack.

**Power 2 - Ether Veil:** Break a measured ampoule to create a small anesthetic cloud. Units remaining inside become slow and drowsy; Clara and prepared allies resist the effect.

**Power 3 - Field Stitch:** Kneel beside a living ally and perform a short interruptible procedure that restores health and removes one wound state.

**Power 4 - Cordial Dose:** Inject an ally with a stimulant that restores action speed and suppresses pain temporarily, followed by visible fatigue when it expires.

**Ultimate - Operating Theatre:** Unfold a compact field table and instrument frame. Inside its small zone, downed allies stabilize, wound procedures accelerate, and Clara may return one recently downed living ally to action if the theatre remains uncontested.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Clara is powerful because her healing occupies real space and time. Exhausting supplies, forcing movement, contaminating the theatre, isolating casualties, or attacking during procedures creates counterplay.

**Visual identity:** A petite adult woman with a beautiful severe face, dark hair pinned practically, fitted ivory field blouse, narrow red waist sash, cropped charcoal medical jacket, split overskirt over trousers, tall boots, leather instrument harness, medical satchel, folding frame, pistol, and visibly used crimson gloves. Provocative through tailoring and confidence, never fetishized as a nurse costume.

## 1.5 Commodore Rosamund Pike - The Blockade Queen

**Concept-art status:** rosamund-pike-v5 is the current proposed revision for review. Completed boarding: lowered pistol, hand on secured capstan, assured small smile. The helm and subordinate sailors establish context without the earlier wrong aiming geometry. This depicts naval surrender, not a new permanent machinery-conversion power.

![Commodore Rosamund Pike - Blockade Queen concept-art candidate](assets/characters/provisional/rosamund-pike-v5.png)

**Trope and narrative:** Rosamund is a naval commander who believes movement is permission granted by whoever controls the water. She built her reputation taking vessels intact, counting every rope and gun before the smoke cleared. She is formal, acquisitive, and personally magnificent in a boarding action. Her conflict with pirates is intimate: both understand prizes, but only one believes paperwork makes seizure lawful.

**Basic action - Boarding Pair:** Fire a heavy boarding pistol, then draw or strike with her naval saber if the target closes.

**Party passive - Deck Discipline:** Allies near Rosamund resist sliding, knockback, panic, and loss of footing on ships, bridges, roofs, and unstable terrain.

**Strategic passive - Closed Waters:** Naval patrols and coastal batteries coordinate interceptions more effectively along sea routes Rosamund has inspected. The effect depends on functioning signals.

**Power 1 - Boarding Hook:** Cast a short naval hook that pulls a light enemy, pulls Rosamund toward a heavy target, or secures her against displacement.

**Power 2 - Signal Turn:** Snap a flag order that lets one ally rotate, reload, and make a short reposition without breaking formation.

**Power 3 - Pistol Brace:** Fire two differently loaded pistols in sequence: the first breaks guard, the second drives the target backward.

**Power 4 - Cut the Line:** Dash along a rope, tether, formation edge, or magical linkage, severing the first removable connection she crosses and striking its owner.

**Ultimate - Clear the Decks:** Rosamund advances through a wide arc with a brace of pistols and a final saber sweep, forcing enemies away from a selected central prize while allies automatically occupy the cleared space.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Rosamund controls crowded platforms and prizes rather than calling an unusable off-map broadside. Long open approaches, dispersed enemies, disarm effects, and fights without a positional objective limit her.

**Visual identity:** A compact, aristocratic adult naval woman in a long deep-blue officer's coat worn open over a fitted cream waistcoat and narrow trousers, gold kept restrained, crimson lining, tall sea boots, boarding saber, brace of pistols, collapsible signal flags, hook line, and waterproof chart case. Beautiful, imperious, and visibly capable of climbing rigging herself.

# 4. Elves: Five Female Heroes

## 4.1 Ariane Leth - The Long Watch

**Trope and narrative:** Ariane has spent decades perfecting shots that other armies would solve by firing a hundred arrows. She is not aloof because she is mystical; she is careful because every elf and every crafted arrow represents time the faction cannot replace quickly. Her calm becomes frightening when she has prepared the ground and selected exactly who must fall.

**Basic action - Longbow:** A slow, precise arrow whose power increases while Ariane maintains line of sight without moving.

**Party passive - Patient Aim:** Party members gain a small accuracy bonus when they delay an attack rather than immediately spending it after movement.

**Strategic passive - Watch the Empty Miles:** Elven observation posts staffed through Ariane's doctrine detect large or careless movements earlier without creating additional scouts.

**Power 1 - Pinning Shot:** Nail a target's clothing, limb guard, wing, or equipment to valid terrain. It immobilizes only while the arrow and anchor remain intact.

**Power 2 - Split Reed:** Fire an arrow that divides after crossing a marked range, striking two nearby light targets with reduced force.

**Power 3 - Leafstep:** Move through prepared living cover to a nearby connected perch, leaving physical disturbance that observant enemies can follow.

**Power 4 - The Necessary Arrow:** Spend one rare crafted arrow selected from a limited field quiver: armor-breaker, silence line, corruption cleanser, or tether cutter.

**Ultimate - Seven Arrows, One Breath:** Ariane fixes her stance and fires seven individually targeted arrows in rapid sequence. Losing line of sight, being displaced, or exhausting valid targets ends the sequence.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Ariane converts patience and preparation into surgical removal. Forced movement, smoke, broken sight lines, expendable decoys, close pressure, and making her waste rare arrows counter her.

**Visual identity:** A tall-for-an-elf but still compact 5-foot-3 adult woman, extremely slim and hardy, beautiful angular face, long ash-blonde braid, fitted moss-gray living-fiber jacket with an open lattice side and narrow waist, split leaf panels over leggings, quiet boots, enormous exquisitely crafted longbow, seven visibly different arrows, and a small pruning knife. No glowing generic fantasy armor.

## 4.2 Mera Syl - The Rootwright

**Trope and narrative:** Mera is an architect whose buildings are alive enough to disagree with her. She coaxes roots into stairs, shields, bridges, braces, and prisons, then returns years later to correct what continued growing. In battle every terrain effect has a seed, root junction, or staff contact that explains where it came from and gives enemies something physical to attack.

**Basic action - Living Staff:** Strike with a shaped staff that plants a small viable root at the impact point when it touches soil or living material.

**Party passive - Living Footing:** Party members standing on Mera's active roots resist slipping, shallow water, mud, and minor forced movement.

**Strategic passive - Rootwright's Plan:** Elven structures connected through routes Mera has shaped require less reconstruction after non-corruptive damage, but the network cannot create people or repair severed magical identity.

**Power 1 - Grow Rampart:** Expand one planted root into a curved waist-high barrier. Fire, salt, axes, and corruption destroy it efficiently.

**Power 2 - Root Snare:** Trigger roots beneath a small target area to bind feet and wheels; stone floors require an existing crack or planted root.

**Power 3 - Lift the Path:** Raise a short ramp, step, or narrow bridge between two valid surfaces, creating a new route usable by both sides.

**Power 4 - Seed Shelter:** Grow a small hollow around one downed ally or vulnerable object, blocking sight while leaving a destructible entrance.

**Ultimate - The Forest Stands Up:** Activate every root Mera planted in the room. Barriers rise, snares tighten, routes connect, and cover turns toward designated threats according to the physical seed layout.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Mera is a construction hero whose power accumulates in a room. Dead stone, fire, salt, rapid relocation, seed destruction, corruption, and enemies exploiting her shared routes counter her.

**Visual identity:** A very short adult elf woman with warm dark skin, wide beautiful eyes, dense black curls threaded with living shoots, fitted bark-brown sleeveless coat, pale-green chest wrap, visible midriff, layered root-fiber panels over narrow trousers, tool belt of seeds and carving blades, and a tall asymmetrical living staff bearing real weight into the ground.

## 4.3 Ione Glassleaf - The Ward-Smith

**Trope and narrative:** Ione is a magical craftswoman who believes a spell that cannot survive handling was never finished. She forges no mass-produced arsenal. Every ward is fitted to a bearer, place, or problem, and every masterpiece consumes time and rare material that population or urgency may not permit her to replace. She is curious about Michael's engineering and offended by anything built to be disposable.

**Basic action - Smith's Hammer:** A short hammer strike that resonates against armor, wards, machines, and crafted objects, revealing one active property.

**Party passive - Well Made:** Equipped party items lose durability and magical stability more slowly while Ione has inspected them during rest.

**Strategic passive - Masterwork Queue:** One elven workshop supervised by Ione may produce a named masterwork with a persistent history instead of additional ordinary output. Losing it is a real content and power loss.

**Power 1 - Inscribe Ward:** Hammer a visible rune plate onto an ally, object, or surface, granting protection against one chosen damage or control family until the plate breaks.

**Power 2 - Unmake Enchantment:** Strike the physical anchor of a magical effect to weaken or remove it. Effects without an accessible anchor resist the action.

**Power 3 - Mirror Bark:** Fit a reflective living plate that returns the first narrow projectile or beam along a readable angle.

**Power 4 - Bind the Charge:** Capture one incoming magical charge inside a prepared vessel, then release it later with reduced power and altered ownership.

**Ultimate - One Perfect Thing:** Assemble a temporary masterwork from Ione's active ward, plate, and captured charge. The resulting item takes one of several forms determined by the ingredients rather than a free menu and lasts until broken or the encounter ends.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Ione turns enemy magic and prepared materials into exact tools. Swarms, unanchored effects, physical pressure, theft of components, broken workshops, and forcing her to spend masterworks on trivial threats counter her.

**Visual identity:** A petite adult elf woman with a cute square face, cropped silver hair, soot-darkened cheek, fitted ivory living-fiber smith's vest with open sides over a dark wrap, narrow reinforced trousers, leaf-metal apron panels, small hammer, rune plates, mirror-bark pieces, charge vessels, carving tools, and exquisitely made bracers. Magical craft, not steampunk ornament.

## 4.4 Tala Ash - The Thornblade

**Trope and narrative:** Tala is the proof that elven hardiness is not another word for hiding. She is a compact frontline duelist who has survived wounds that would have forced younger armies to replace her. She fights economically, uses living armor as a moving brace, and retreats before pride turns a recoverable wound into permanent attrition.

**Basic action - Thornblade:** A curved two-handed blade cut with a narrow arc and strong guard damage.

**Party passive - Hard to Fell:** Once per encounter, the first party member pushed below a critical health threshold receives a brief window to move toward cover before becoming fully downed.

**Strategic passive - Veteran Memory:** Elven trainees assigned near Tala gain discipline and defensive technique more quickly, but the benefit represents teaching and cannot reproduce her accumulated hero experience.

**Power 1 - Barkskin Step:** Grow living plates across Tala's leading side as she advances, reducing frontal damage but slowing turns.

**Power 2 - Thorn Riposte:** Enter a guarded stance; blocking a close attack drives controlled thorns into the attacker's weapon arm rather than dealing indiscriminate area damage.

**Power 3 - Stag Leap:** Vault from prepared root, cover, or elevation and deliver a downward blade strike. Without a valid launch surface the range is short.

**Power 4 - Sapblood:** Press a living resin seal into one wound, converting ongoing bleeding into reduced movement until the seal is safely removed.

**Ultimate - Until the Grove Moves:** Tala anchors her feet and claims a compact zone. Each successful block grows her living armor and extends her blade reach slightly; leaving the zone or being uprooted ends the accumulation.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Tala is a narrow durable anchor, not an immortal tank. Rear attacks, fire, salt, forced movement, ranged kiting, wide flanks, and making her abandon the claimed zone counter her.

**Visual identity:** A 5-foot-1 lean adult elf woman with a beautiful strong jaw, short dark-green-black hair, fitted charcoal living-leather top with a visible central waist, flexible bark plates over one shoulder and hip, narrow leggings, quiet armored boots, resin seals, and a long curved thornblade. Athletic and hardened without bulk or exaggerated breasts.

## 4.5 Neris Ond - The Memory Gardener

**Trope and narrative:** Neris cultivates memories as carefully as other people cultivate food. She records voice, touch, habit, and relationship in living vessels so that recovery returns a person rather than a convenient body. She is warm, sensual, and deeply present because forgetting is the enemy she actually fears. Her magic cannot make a new veteran; it can save the one who is falling out of reach.

**Basic action - Seed Sling:** Launch a hard seed that bursts into a small patch of medicinal or revealing pollen on impact.

**Party passive - Remembered Name:** Party members recovered from downed states retain more temporary buffs, summoned bonds, and encounter memory rather than returning in a blank reset state.

**Strategic passive - Long Recovery:** Rebirth sites holding Neris's prepared memory vessels have better odds of returning the correct identity and relationships. This preserves existing people and does not accelerate sanctuary generation.

**Power 1 - Memory Thread:** Tie a luminous plant fiber between Neris and one ally. If the ally is displaced, concealed, or downed, the thread shows the physical route toward her.

**Power 2 - Pollen Veil:** Crush a cultivated pod into a small cloud that reveals breathing living creatures while obscuring exact silhouettes at range.

**Power 3 - Graft the Wound:** Apply living tissue to one ally over a short channel, converting a severe wound into a removable graft burden that reduces maximum speed until treated after combat.

**Power 4 - Recall the Body:** Pull a marked downed ally or recoverable body a short distance through connected living ground. Dead stone or severed roots stop the movement.

**Ultimate - Garden of Returning:** Grow a temporary enclosed recovery garden around marked allies and bodies. Downed allies stabilize, identity corruption slows, and one recoverable ally may stand again if Neris remains inside maintaining the garden.

**Play pattern and counterplay:** Neris is a physical identity-and-recovery hero. Fire, blight, severed roots, forced movement, destroyed vessels, rapid lethal finishing attacks, and contamination of the garden counter her.

**Visual identity:** A tiny adult elf woman with a soft beautiful face, warm brown skin, long pale-gold hair woven with memory fibers, fitted deep-green wrap top, openwork sleeves, visible belly, narrow layered trousers, seed sling, pruning knife, living satchel, luminous thread spools, graft vessels, and a small memory terrarium carried in a protective harness.

# 5. Art Generation Contract

Each hero receives an individual full-body production portrait after her design is complete. The portrait must express her narrative, basic loop, signature equipment, faction materials, and tactical silhouette without names, labels, interface frames, or typography. The first image pass is provisional concept art. Nothing becomes an installed character photograph until specifically approved.

The four faction sets must remain recognizable with palette removed:

- Colonial women read through disciplined equipment, institutional roles, standardized weapons, formations, records, and practical 1870s authority.
- Fox women read through individual relationship tokens, tails and ears, masks, lanterns, routes, living social warmth, and rule-governed deception.
- Pirate women read through prize-taking equipment, ropes, capture tools, gold contracts, brig keys, jury-rigged foreign hardware, and shipboard mobility.
- Elven women read through scarce masterwork equipment, living materials, exceptional maintenance, deliberate magic anchors, survival tools, and no disposable mass-production language.
