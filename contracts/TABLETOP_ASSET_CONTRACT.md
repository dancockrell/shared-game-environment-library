# Shared 2D asset contract

Owner direction, 8 September 2026: all projects use 2D artwork only. Do not acquire, generate, admit, restore or reuse 3D models, rigs, model builders, volumetric scenery or model-only material packs. The owner requested permanent deletion, including shared packs and archived copies.

Every deliverable records pixel dimensions, intended camera perspective, palette/style family, transparent bounds and ground/contact pivot. Sprites additionally record ordered frame regions, timings, action and facing, playback mode and recovery pose. Foot positions and equipment hands must remain stable.

Scenes and effects must use the same perspective and pixel density as characters. Shared art remains independent of game rules; authoritative game state controls presentation. Admission requires native rendered inspection as well as valid metadata.

Mesh scale, skeletons, collision hulls, LODs and PBR maps are no longer asset requirements. Existing historical catalogs must not be interpreted as permission to revive that workflow.
